HelloFresh Recipe #47 • Tonight's Special

Easy Peasy Spinach & Ricotta Tortelloni

with Creamy Pesto Sauce, Peas and Mushrooms

⏱️ 10–15 Minutes 🥗 Veggie 🍎 1 of your 5 a day 👨‍🍳 Chef: Steve

🛒 Shopping List

  • 🥟 Spinach & Ricotta Tortelloni (300g)
  • 🍄 Sliced Mushrooms (120g)
  • 🥛 Crème Fraîche (150g)
  • 🥣 Vegetable Stock Paste (10g)
  • 🌿 Basil Pesto with Cashew Nuts (30g)
  • 🟢 Peas (120g)
  • 🧂 Salt, Pepper, Olive Oil & Water (50ml)

🍳 Cooking Tools

Frying Pan, Kettle, Medium Saucepan

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Method

1
Tortelloni Time

Boil a full kettle. Pour into saucepan with 1/2 tsp salt. Boil tortelloni for 4–5 mins. Once cooked, drain and drizzle with oil to prevent sticking.

2
Sauce Time

Heat a drizzle of oil in a frying pan on high heat. Fry mushrooms for 5–6 mins, season with salt & pepper. Stir through crème fraîche, veg stock paste, pesto, peas, and water (50ml). Simmer for 1–2 mins.

3
Bring Together

Add the cooked tortelloni to the pesto sauce. Stir gently to combine. Season with salt and pepper to taste. (Add a splash of water if sauce is too thick).

4
Dinner's Ready! 🎉

Serve the piping hot tortelloni in bowls and enjoy!
